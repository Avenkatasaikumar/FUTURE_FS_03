# Website Proposal: Brew & Bloom Café (Concept Demo)

**Prepared by:** Aggala Venkata Sai Kumar
**Live demo:** https://avenkatasaikumar.github.io/FUTURE_FS_03/
*This is a concept proposal using sample business details. It shows what a local café would receive and can be adapted to any real business.*

## 1. The business
Brew & Bloom is a neighbourhood café serving freshly roasted coffee, daily bakes and light meals. Its customers are students, remote workers, families and friends who want a relaxed place to sit.

## 2. The problem
A customer who searches for the café online often cannot quickly find:
- opening hours and the exact location
- the menu and prices
- an easy way to book a table or ask a question

People who can't find this in a few seconds usually go to a competitor who shows it clearly.

## 3. The solution
A fast, mobile-friendly website with:
- **Home:** a clear promise and a one-tap *Call* button
- **Menu:** categorised items and prices, easy to update
- **About:** the café's story, which builds trust
- **Reviews:** social proof from happy guests
- **Contact:** address, hours, embedded map, directions and WhatsApp booking

## 4. How it helps attract customers
| Benefit | Result |
|---|---|
| Appears in Google searches | New customers can find the café |
| Professional look | Visitors trust it before they arrive |
| WhatsApp and call buttons | More bookings with less effort |
| Menu always online | Fewer repeat questions for staff |
| Works on every phone | Most customers browse on mobile |

## 6. Why this approach
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript means the site loads fast, costs nothing to host and has no plugins to maintain or secure.
